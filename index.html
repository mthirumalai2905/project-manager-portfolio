About

I am a project management professional working across business operations and cross-functional delivery. The standard is the same on a SaaS launch, a partner offer, or an AI workflow: a requirement people agree on, a sequence with owners, a launch, and a next version based on what the operation actually did.

Governance, milestones, dependencies, and a RAID log when the work is large. Sprints and a visible board when the team needs a cadence. Status reporting and presentation when stakeholders need the same story. Change management and SOPs when a new workflow has to survive after the kickoff.
